  than they save, with LLY as the first case. The rule stays until the 5-day evidence is in; LAURUSLABS (India) is the
  next case to record, and NYKAA/ZYDUSLIFE (4 Nov) and AAPL/AMGN/MET (2-4 Nov) will test it either way.
